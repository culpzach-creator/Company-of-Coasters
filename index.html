Facility coverage guide

Each land needs an operational restroom and restaurant, regardless of distance. A land loses 10% of its points per missing amenity (up to 20%). Each park also needs guest services (-5% park score if missing). Above 1M attendance, keep at least 25% of points in support and scenery to avoid overcrowding penalties.
